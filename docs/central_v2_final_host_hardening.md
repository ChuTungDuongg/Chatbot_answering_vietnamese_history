# Central V2 final host hardening

This pass changes only the general host path for relational events, target selection, supported explanation depth and citation recovery. It uses the current checkout and no historical answer table.

1. **Lost actor:** the old cause grammar captured actors around outcome predicates such as failure, but not around agreement or diplomatic actions. Its abbreviation fallback could recognize the first actor while losing a coordinated second actor.
2. **Coordinated extraction:** bounded named-entity captures now support `và`, `với`, `giữa … và …`, and `cả … lẫn …`, optional controller verbs and organization prefixes. Every conjunct must qualify; ordinary or mixed noun lists are not partly promoted into actors. Existing aliases are reused.
3. **Events:** reusable predicates identify normalization, establishment/severance of relations, agreements, negotiation, military withdrawal/participation and political state changes. Actions retain bounded objects such as agreement names. A noun inside an actor name does not outrank a later valid predicate.
4. **Canonical planning:** analysis carries `event_type` and `canonical_target`. The leading retrieval variant contains event, actor set and requested facet; the raw question remains a bounded fallback. Recovery uses the same target. Event terminology alone does not become an explicitly requested analytical facet.
5. **Discarded anchors:** the missing event left the old selector without a specific bilateral target. Its greedy dimension gain could then prefer contextual material over highly ranked direct sources, especially after its first overview reservation.
6. **Selector priority:** normalized action/object and actor overlap establish direct event coverage. Exact or near-exact titles, including existing actor aliases and nominal agreement titles, receive anchor value. Canonical event metadata used for selection is preserved in the synthesis source heading. A bounded joint event sentence can establish direct coverage without an exact title.
7. **Relevance before diversity:** joint target consistency precedes causal/requested-facet relevance, source quality and batch-local retrieval rank; marginal dimensions are considered afterward. A conflicting actor pair in the actual excerpt defeats title-only joint coverage. Chronology without explanatory cues cannot establish causal sufficiency.
8. **Actor coverage:** `required_actor_coverage`, `selected_actor_coverage`, `unresolved_actor_scopes` and `direct_target_coverage` are exposed. Both requested actors must be covered. Separate actor evidence is tracked but conservatively does not establish a complete joint-event answer by itself.
9. **Depth:** focused explanations default to at least two expressed dimensions when three are strongly supported; broad analysis keeps three when four are supported. Both policies are configurable; simple facts have no analytical requirement. Only selected causal excerpts count, and the event label itself does not supply a factor. The focused synthesis contract expands with evidence breadth: central thesis, distinct factors, mechanisms and contribution to the event, without a fixed essay length.
10. **Summary versus fact:** a short concluding paragraph may inherit support from immediately preceding cited paragraphs only when those paragraphs are independently supported and each conclusion clause is an ordered restatement. Novel entities, years, negation and predicates retain the uncited-new-fact classification. A summary cue alone is insufficient.
11. **Recovery:** high-confidence deterministic alignment runs before model citation mapping, respects event/actor targets and rejects new capitalized entities. Existing citations to the wrong relation are also flagged. Every attempt is revalidated; unchanged citation counts report no progress. One remaining uncited paragraph in an otherwise cited answer can receive the existing single bounded full rewrite after mapping fails. Unsupported answers still fail closed after recovery is exhausted.
12. **Proper-noun audit:** no new production historical entity, article title, factual answer or question-specific branch was added. Historical names appear only in regression fixtures; runtime normalization reuses the existing alias metadata.
13. **Verification:** the Central and related local suite passed **447 tests**, including **51 relational-host cases**. It covers parser variants, ordinary conjunct rejection, direct/other-pair ranking, chronology, actor completeness, metadata preservation, excerpt-only breadth, configurable coverage, compressed-summary/new-fact classification, deterministic alignment and bounded recovery. `python -m compileall -q app tests` and `git diff --check` passed. Tests used fake runtimes with Hugging Face offline and CUDA hidden.
14. **Remaining limits:** this is bounded Vietnamese grammar and conservative lexical support, not unrestricted named-entity recognition or semantic entailment. Implicit actors, unusual name capitalization, distant cross-sentence relations and loose paraphrases may remain unresolved or require a bounded repair. Source quality is not independently verified bibliographically. No live model behavior, adapter quality or production latency was measured.
15. **Freeze readiness:** the requested host regressions and compatibility checks pass. The runtime is ready to freeze for the next Adapter V2 training phase under these local gates. Base-versus-adapter evaluation remains a later phase.

No benchmark or metrics framework was created. OCR, clipboard, attachment flow, frontend branding/UI, Modal deployment, artifact lifecycle and the Central adapter loading contract were not modified by this pass. Base Qwen3-8B and the future Central V2 adapter hook remain supported. No model training, Modal command, GPU inference, model download, dataset processing or Volume mutation was executed. Unrelated workspace README/image edits were left untouched.
